Frequently Asked Questions

Expanding an accordion fires an engagement event in GA4.

What is the Google Analytics 4 Realtime report? +
The Realtime report shows activity on your site as it happens in the last 30 minutes, allowing you to instantly verify tag installations, monitor campaigns, and inspect event parameters.
How are conversions tracked in Google Analytics 4? +
In GA4, every conversion is an event. You can mark any triggered event (such as generate_lead or purchase) as a conversion in the GA4 Admin > Events panel.
How do traffic sources get identified in GA4? +
GA4 automatically records referral headers, document referrers, and URL parameters such as utm_source, utm_medium, and utm_campaign.
